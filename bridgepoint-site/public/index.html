Questions employers ask us

Can't see your question? Call us on (02) 9248 7294.

What types of roles do you recruit for?

Logistics and operations roles, from warehouse, transport and production staff through to supervisors, managers and directors, plus the finance, HR, payroll, customer service and admin teams that support them.

How do your fees work?

Fees depend on the service: permanent, executive search or temporary. They are agreed in writing before we start. Permanent fees are payable only when your new hire starts, and volume rates apply from two roles.

What happens if a candidate does not work out?

Permanent placements carry a 6-month replacement guarantee. If the hire leaves within that period, we find a replacement at no additional fee. Conditions apply, including payment within 14 days of invoice.

How do you find the right candidates?

We start with a detailed brief on the role, the site and your team, then search our candidate pool and the wider market, screen and interview, and present a shortlist of candidates who fit.

How involved can we be in the process?

As involved as you like, from reviewing every profile to joining interviews.

Can we convert a temp to permanent?

Yes. Conversion terms are set out in writing before the temporary assignment begins.
