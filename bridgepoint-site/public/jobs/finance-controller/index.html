Finance Controller

Lead financial control, reporting and compliance for an established, innovative mining company.

About the role

A well-established mining company known for innovation is seeking a Finance Controller to oversee financial operations, ensure accuracy and drive strategic planning as the business grows.

On offer: a competitive salary and benefits, a collaborative environment, and genuine professional development.

What you will do

  • Oversee budgeting, forecasting and financial reporting
  • Lead the accounting team through transactions, reconciliations and month-end close
  • Ensure compliance with accounting standards and internal policy
  • Analyse performance and trends to support strategic decisions
  • Prepare P&L, balance sheet and cash flow reporting for senior management
  • Improve financial processes and systems
  • Manage internal and external audits

What you will bring

  • Degree in finance, accounting or a related field
  • CA, CPA or equivalent highly regarded
  • Significant experience in financial control
  • Proven finance team leadership
  • Advanced Excel and financial systems skills
