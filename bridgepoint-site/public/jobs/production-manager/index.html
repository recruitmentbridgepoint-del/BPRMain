Production Manager

Own end-to-end production for a sustainability-focused fashion brand, from raw materials to finished goods.

About the role

A sustainability-focused fashion brand is seeking a Production Manager to oversee manufacturing from start to finish. You will deliver on-time, cost-effective production that meets the brand's standards, while leading the production team and managing suppliers.

What you will do

  • Lead and develop the production team with a focus on continuous improvement
  • Build production schedules aligned to demand forecasts
  • Oversee production from sourcing through to finished goods
  • Manage supplier performance and corrective actions
  • Work with QC on quality procedures
  • Manage the production budget and reduce waste
  • Coordinate with inventory and logistics teams on stock levels
  • Ensure WHS compliance, training and audits

What you will bring

  • 5–7 years in production management, ideally in fashion or premium retail
  • Strong knowledge of production, quality control and supply chain
  • Proven team leadership
  • Data-driven decision-making
  • Strong supplier relationship and communication skills
